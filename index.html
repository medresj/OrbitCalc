Information

This calculator calculates the theoretical mass of a payload based on the efficiency of the SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket.

As of now it is just a purely theoretical model, which 1) assumes 100% energy efficiency, which is pysically impossible, 2) assumes that the rocket doesn't have any mass, and 3) should (of course) not be used for anything serious.

If you plan on using the "custom" option, please know that it is even more inaccurate.
