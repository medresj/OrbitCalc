
  what i want to do here
==========================

I want to create a easy to use calculator for getting into into orbit, more precisely: low earth orbit.
My goal is to create something similar to https://www.overvieweffekt.com/tools/relativistic-travel-calculator
I also want the user to be able to input joules of energy and it gives you the most weight you could theoretically get into LEO.
Why: I once wondered how much mass i could get into Orbit with the energy which was written at the back of food and beverages (a white Monster for example has 50kj of energy).
